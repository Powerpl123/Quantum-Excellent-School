Our School

A Strong Foundation for Bright Futures

At Quantum Excellent School, we guide children to become confident communicators, critical thinkers, and responsible citizens. Our approach blends child-centered teaching, strong academic standards, and values-based learning.

From Nursery to Primary, each stage is carefully designed to support growth in literacy, numeracy, creativity, social skills, and leadership.

Academics

Our Learning Pathway

Nursery School

Play-based and inquiry-driven learning for early development.

  • Language and phonics development
  • Numeracy through activities and games
  • Music, movement, and social confidence

Lower Primary

Core subject mastery and confidence-building classroom routines.

  • English, Mathematics, and Science
  • Reading fluency and writing skills
  • Character and teamwork activities

Upper Primary

Academic depth, digital literacy, and leadership preparation.

  • STEM and project-based learning
  • Research, communication, and leadership
  • Preparation for secondary education

Admissions

Begin Your Child's Journey With Us

Admissions are open for Nursery and Primary classes. We offer a warm, structured learning environment where every child is guided to grow in knowledge, confidence, and character.

  1. Book a school visit or call our admissions office.
  2. Submit learner details and required documents.
  3. Attend age-appropriate assessment and parent briefing.
  4. Receive offer letter and complete registration.
